Vietnam: when your child's Korean father disappears

The same Korean court process applies to Vietnamese mothers, whether you live in Vietnam or in Korea.

Follow the 6 steps. A Vietnamese version of this guide is here.

If you live in Korea

  • Call Danuri 1577-1366 and choose Vietnamese. It is free, 24 hours a day, and can connect you to free lawyer consultations.
  • Low-income foreign residents can apply to the Korea Legal Aid Corporation (132) for free representation.
  • If you were married to him in Korea, the divorce court can decide custody and child support at the same time.

If you live in Vietnam

  • Contact the Korean Embassy in Hanoi or the Consulate General in Ho Chi Minh City for document questions.
  • A Korean lawyer can file the case for you; you may not need to travel. Ask whether DNA samples can be collected in Vietnam.
  • Prepare your child's birth certificate (Giấy khai sinh) with a certified translation.
