About ParentRight Korea

What we do

We explain, in plain language, the official legal routes for children of Korean fathers to be recognized and supported.

What we never do

  • We never publish names, photos, contact details or any information about any person.
  • We do not accept posts, comments or uploads.
  • We do not give legal advice and are not a law firm.
  • We do not refer cases to lawyers for money.

Privacy

This site has no accounts and does not collect personal data. The "Find help" questions run only inside your browser; answers are never sent or stored. If analytics or ads are added later, this page will be updated first.

Corrections

If something is wrong or out of date, please tell us at contact@parentright.example. Please do not send us personal information about anyone.
