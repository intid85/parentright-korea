# Outreach posts

Drafts for sharing ParentRight Korea in existing communities and with support organizations. The owner posts and sends these personally; nothing here is sent automatically.

## Where to share
- **Support organizations** (message or email, one at a time): We Love Kopino (WLK), Kopino Children Association, multicultural family support centers in Korea (다문화가족지원센터), migrant support NGOs. Contact them only through their official pages.
- **Facebook groups** for Filipino or Vietnamese mothers, Filipinas or Vietnamese in Korea, and single mothers. Find them by searching Facebook for terms like "Kopino", "Filipina in Korea", "OFW Korea", "single mom Philippines", "Phụ nữ Việt tại Hàn Quốc", "mẹ đơn thân".
- **Korean communities**: only where the topic fits (multicultural family, single-parent or social-welfare communities).

## Rules before posting
- Read each group's rules first. If promotion or links are not allowed, ask an admin before posting.
- Post in one group at a time, a few days apart. Never paste the same post into many groups at once (spam reports can get the account blocked).
- Never mention or reply about a specific father, case or person. If someone shares a story in the comments, point them to free help (Danuri 1577-1366, legal aid 132) instead of discussing it.
- Do not add the Ko-fi link to these posts.
- Do not offer to handle anyone's case or recommend a lawyer.

## Facebook group post: English (Philippines and general)

**Is your child's father Korean and he stopped supporting you?**

There is a free guide that explains, step by step, how mothers outside Korea can:
- prove paternity in a Korean family court (you can do this while living abroad)
- claim monthly child support and support for past years
- get Korean nationality for the child

It also lists free help in Korea (Danuri helpline in Tagalog and English, legal aid) and a short quiz that shows where to go for your situation. Nothing you answer is saved.

👉 https://parentright.org
Tagalog version: https://parentright.org/tl/

It is free, independent, and does not ask for your name or any personal information. It is general information, not legal advice.

Please share with anyone who needs it. 🙏

## Facebook group post: Tagalog

**Koreano ba ang ama ng anak mo at tumigil na siyang sumuporta?**

May libreng gabay na nagpapaliwanag, hakbang-hakbang, kung paano ang mga inang nasa labas ng Korea ay puwedeng:
- patunayan sa korte ng Korea na siya ang ama (puwede kahit nasa Pilipinas ka)
- humingi ng buwanang sustento at sustento para sa mga nakaraang taon
- makakuha ng Korean citizenship ang anak mo

May listahan din ng libreng tulong (Danuri helpline na may Tagalog, legal aid) at maikling quiz kung saan ka dapat lumapit. Walang sine-save na sagot.

👉 https://parentright.org/tl/

Libre ito, independiyente, at hindi humihingi ng pangalan o personal na impormasyon. Pangkalahatang impormasyon ito, hindi legal na payo.

Paki-share sa mga nangangailangan. 🙏

## Facebook group post: Vietnamese

**Bố của con bạn là người Hàn Quốc và đã ngừng chu cấp?**

Có một trang hướng dẫn miễn phí giải thích từng bước cách các mẹ có thể:
- yêu cầu tòa án Hàn Quốc công nhận cha con (có thể làm khi đang ở nước ngoài)
- yêu cầu tiền cấp dưỡng hằng tháng và cho những năm trước
- giúp con có quốc tịch Hàn Quốc

Trang cũng có danh sách hỗ trợ miễn phí (tổng đài Danuri có tiếng Việt, trợ giúp pháp lý).

👉 https://parentright.org/vi/

Trang miễn phí, độc lập, không hỏi tên hay thông tin cá nhân. Đây là thông tin chung, không phải tư vấn pháp lý.

Hãy chia sẻ cho người cần. 🙏

## Message to a support organization: English

Subject: Free guide for mothers of children with Korean fathers

Hello,

I run ParentRight Korea (https://parentright.org), a free and independent information guide for mothers abroad whose child's father is Korean. It explains in plain English, Tagalog and Vietnamese how to pursue paternity acknowledgment, child support and Korean nationality through official Korean channels, and lists free help such as Danuri (1577-1366) and the Korea Legal Aid Corporation (132).

The site:
- never publishes information about any individual
- has no comments, uploads or forms, and collects no personal data
- does not give legal advice or refer cases to lawyers for a fee
- cites its legal sources, and corrections can be sent to contact@parentright.org

If it is useful for the mothers you work with, you are welcome to share it. I would also be grateful for any corrections from your experience.

Thank you for your work.

ParentRight Korea
contact@parentright.org
